A LITTLE ABOUT ME


Pronouns: She/Her

Multi-disciplinary Digital Artist, UI/UX Designer, and Technical Specialist with a B.A. in Digital Art & Design (Cum Laude) from the University of West Florida. She combines visual asset creation, programming, and user-centered design principles using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, C++, and Adobe Creative Suite to build intuitive interfaces, responsive web applications, and custom game mechanics.

Beyond digital design, Sydney brings hands-on fabrication and technical leadership experience as a certified welder who has supervised university wood/metal labs and designed 3D stage environments. Drawing from her work as a freelance web designer, tattoo artist, and former studio location manager, she effectively leads end-to-end project lifecycles from concept and consultation through final execution.

THE REAL WHY

I'm a creative - not just 'I draw', no I create beyond the paper. New people, new ideas. I build, I learn, I craft, and I make possible. And if it's something I don't know how to do, I teach myself how to. I'm a restless soul. I can't be doing nothing - it would drive me mad if I didn't use my time I have living for creating and experiences.
I want to be someone that's remembered, not awarded. I don't need anyone to tell me I 'did a good job', I want people to be affected by me in a way. To influence others to be creative - to turn myself into a motivation for the restless.
